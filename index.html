🦉 Welcome to Unstuck

A Clearer Thinking prototype. When you're stuck on something, this tool gets you moving in a few small steps: a quick one-minute technique matched to what's holding you back, naming the task, picking one small thing you could do in the next 10 minutes, then a timer to help you go do it.

No account, no judgment. Just come back when the timer's done, and do it again. An owl keeps you company and grows a little more with every round.
