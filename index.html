🦉 Welcome to Unstuck

A Clearer Thinking prototype. When you're stuck procrastinating on something, this tool gets you moving in three quick steps: name the task, name one small thing you could do in the next 10 minutes, then start a timer and go do it.

No account, no judgment — just come back when the timer's done and do it again. An owl keeps you company and grows a little each round.
